Privacy Policy

Verdure: Scan & PDF Studio
Effective Date: September 12, 2026  •  Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Overview

Verdure: Scan & PDF Studio ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. Your documents, scans, and photos belong solely to you.

Verdure is designed as a standalone, on-device document and image utility. This Privacy Policy explains what the App accesses, why, and what it does — and does not — do with that access.

2. On-Device Processing & Zero Data Collection

Verdure operates using native Apple frameworks (including VisionKit, PhotosUI, CoreImage, PDFKit, and CoreGraphics) and performs all scanning, filtering, resizing, compression, format conversion, and PDF assembly directly on your device.

  • No External Uploads: Your scans, photos, and generated PDF documents are processed entirely locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or backed up to our servers or any third-party cloud infrastructure.
  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location.
  • No Account Required: You can use all core scanning, adjustment, conversion, and PDF-building features immediately without creating an account or logging in.

3. Camera Access (Document Scanner)

  • Purpose: Verdure requests camera access solely to power its built-in document scanner (Apple's VisionKit document camera), which detects document edges and captures pages when you choose to scan.
  • User-Initiated Only: The camera is only activated when you tap "Start Scanning." Verdure does not access the camera in the background or without your explicit action.
  • No Image Transmission: Scanned pages remain on your device and are only shared if you explicitly use the share sheet to send them elsewhere.

4. Photo Library Access

  • Explicit User Selection: Verdure uses Apple's modern PhotosPicker framework. The App only accesses the specific photo or photos you intentionally select for editing, converting, resizing, or adding to a PDF.
  • No Background Library Scanning: The App does not index, scan, or monitor your broader photo library. PhotosPicker runs in a separate, sandboxed system process that never reports your full library contents to the App.

5. Local Settings Storage

Verdure stores a small number of preferences (such as whether haptic feedback is enabled and your default export quality) locally on your device using Apple's standard settings storage (UserDefaults). This information never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

6. Saving & Sharing Outputs

When you choose to export or share a processed image or PDF document, Verdure uses the standard iOS/iPadOS Share Sheet, which lets you save the file to your device, save it to Photos, or send it through any app or service you choose. Verdure does not control or have visibility into what happens to a file after you share it through the system share sheet.

7. Third-Party Services & Tracking

  • Zero Third-Party SDKs: Verdure contains no third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.
  • No Advertising: The App does not integrate any third-party ad networks, tracking identifiers (IDFA), or advertising cookies.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

8. Children's Privacy

Verdure does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App does not collect personal data, there is no personal information about a child for us to access, retain, or disclose.

9. Security

Your data security is inherently supported by Apple's iOS/iPadOS application sandboxing architecture. All scanning, editing, and file transformations execute inside the App's secure local environment and are discarded or saved only upon your direct action.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates to Verdure. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
